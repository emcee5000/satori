# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Satori to be a friendly, welcoming project for everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or
invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Things that help:

- Being kind and patient, especially with newcomers
- Respecting different viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what's best for the project and its users

Things that aren't acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour by opening a private
[security advisory](https://github.com/emcee5000/satori/security/advisories/new) or by contacting the maintainer
through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or
contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors who do so.
